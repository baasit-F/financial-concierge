Common questions

How much life insurance do I need?

A common starting point is 10–15 times your annual income, but the real answer depends on your debts, your family's future expenses and what you already have. I'll walk through it with you for free.

Is term or permanent life insurance better?

Neither is "better" — they solve different problems. Term is cost-effective protection for a specific period; permanent coverage lasts your whole life and may build cash value. Many families use a combination.

Can a small business offer group health benefits?

Yes. Many carriers offer group plans for businesses with as few as two employees, and there are often more options than owners expect.
