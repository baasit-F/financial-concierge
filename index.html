Quick answers

Does it cost anything to get a quote?

No. Quotes and consultations are 100% free with no obligation.

How much life insurance do I need?

A common starting point is 10–15× your income, but debts, kids and goals all matter. Try the calculator above, then Ben will fine-tune it with you.

Term or permanent, which is better?

They solve different problems. Term is budget-friendly protection for a set period. Permanent lasts your whole life and may build cash value. Many families use both.

Can a small business offer group benefits?

Yes. Many carriers offer group plans for businesses with as few as two employees.

Do I need a medical exam?

Not always. Many carriers now offer policies with simplified or no-exam underwriting for qualifying applicants.
